## A pavilion stay in Karuizawa

HOSHINOYA Karuizawa arranges its accommodation as guest pavilions. Mizunami rooms face the river, while Yamaroji is another category with a different setting. Choose by room description rather than assuming every pavilion has the same outlook.

## Time for dining and bathing

The resort overview explicitly lists two onsen facilities and Wi-Fi in guest rooms. Its dining guide includes Japanese breakfast and in-room dining options, with reservations required for the arrangements described. Check meal inclusions when selecting a rate.

## Arriving from Tokyo

The official access guide describes taking the Hokuriku Shinkansen to Karuizawa and continuing by taxi or local bus to the Hoshino Area. For any hotel transfer, confirm the current station and pickup point directly: the overview and dedicated access page differ.

## Before reserving

The listed address is 2148 Nagakura, Karuizawa-machi, Nagano. Verify room layout, transport arrangements and any bathing or accessibility requirements with the property. This guide is based on published sources rather than a personal stay.
