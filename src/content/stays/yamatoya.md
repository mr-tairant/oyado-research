## A ryokan at the heart of Dogo

Yamatoya Honten stands beside Dogo Onsen Honkan in Matsuyama. Founded in 1868, the inn combines Japanese rooms shaped by tea-room architecture with Western-style alternatives.

## Rooms, dining and bathing

Choose a room category deliberately: the options differ in layout and bedding. The official site describes Japanese multi-course dinners, indoor and outdoor hot-spring baths, and a lounge for relaxing after a soak. Confirm what your chosen rate includes.

## Getting there

Dogo Onsen tram station is approximately five minutes away on foot. The address is 20-8 Dogo Yunomachi, Matsuyama, Ehime. This guide concerns the Dogo property, not the similarly named inn in Osaka.

## Before reserving

Use the property's English website for current details. Ask directly about dietary needs, bathing rules and accessibility before booking. This is a source-based guide; OYADO has not conducted an owner interview or claimed a personal stay.
