## Zao Onsen, with a change ahead

KIRAKU ONSEN LIVING is in Zao Onsen, Yamagata. Its official website describes indoor and outdoor hot-spring baths.

## Match the room to your dates

Checked on 26 October 2026, the site distinguishes its existing rooms for stays through October from new accommodation beginning 1 January 2027. Apartments and other announced room types belong to that future phase, not to a promise about today's facilities. Confirm operation and room availability for your dates directly.

## Dining during the transition

The current dining section says meals are not served. Proposed ground-floor dining and cafe/bar spaces are future announcements. Plan meals separately unless your booking explicitly confirms otherwise.

## Getting there

The official route is approximately 45 minutes by bus from JR Yamagata Station, then a seven-minute walk from Zao Onsen Bus Terminal. The address is 935-25 Zao Onsen, Yamagata City. Transport details and renewal plans should be checked again before travel.
