## An island stay with a sense of place

At HOSHINOYA Taketomi Island, villas reference the island's architectural guidelines through low stone fences and coral-sand yards. Living areas form part of the accommodation. A pool is explicitly listed in the resort overview.

## Dining in the Yaeyama Islands

The Island Terroir dinner takes a French approach to ingredients from the Yaeyama Islands. Reserve ahead through the property and check meal inclusions separately from your room rate.

## Plan the journey in stages

Travel first to Ishigaki Island, then continue from Ishigaki Port by ferry to Taketomi Port. The hotel's shuttle connects the port and resort. The official access guide gives approximate journey times; they are not a ferry timetable.

## Before reserving

Check current ferry operations, especially when weather affects sailings. The property is at 1955 Taketomi, Taketomi-cho, Okinawa. This guide does not imply that the resort has an onsen, a private pool or a particular language service unless separately confirmed.
