## Arashiyama from the riverside

HOSHINOYA Kyoto is a riverside retreat in Arashiyama. Its guest pavilions look toward the gorge, and the official overview lists 25 rooms. The setting makes the arrival arrangements part of planning a stay.

## Arrange the final leg

The hotel's dedicated access guide describes a boat transfer from its waiting area to the accommodation, with advance reservation required. Weather and river conditions may change that arrangement. Follow the instructions supplied with your confirmed booking; do not assume the resort address is a direct taxi drop-off.

## Rooms and dining

Wi-Fi is listed in guest rooms and some public areas. The dining guide describes Japanese breakfast served in-room with prior reservation, alongside separate dinner options. Check the meal arrangements attached to your selected rate.

## Before reserving

The property lists no hotel parking. Confirm transport and any specific dietary or accessibility requirements before booking. This source-based guide does not claim an onsen, pool or owner interview, and it does not imply a personal visit.
